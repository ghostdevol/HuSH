# HuSH Site Rules

Version 1.0 — October 15, 2026

HuSH is a small, private-feeling social app: profiles, a feed, chat rooms, direct messages, video, and cypher rooms where people perform. These rules keep it that way. They are enforced by the server, not by vibes — breaking them has real consequences, up to a permanent ban.

## 1. One person, one account

- You get **one account**. Registration requires an email address and a phone number, and each can only be attached to one account. Accounts are also tracked by IP address for abuse prevention.
- **Double accounts are banned permanently.** If we find duplicate accounts — the same person running more than one — the duplicates are banned, their email, phone number, and IP address are blacklisted, and they cannot re-register.
- The one exception: a genuine mistake or a server error. Those cases are reviewed by the admin, and the admin can lift a ban that should never have landed. There is no public unban form — the exception exists so innocent people are not punished for our mistakes, not as a loophole for evasion.

## 2. Consent is built in — respect it

- **Nobody owes you a conversation.** Your first direct message to someone is a *request*. It only becomes a conversation if they accept it. A denial is final — do not re-ask through other accounts, rooms, or comments.
- **Video calls work the same way.** Starting video in a DM sends an invitation. Media only connects when the other person accepts. Do not spam call invitations.
- **Blocking is a first-class right.** If someone blocks you, you cannot message them, follow them, see their content, or reach them on video — and you will not be told details. Accept it and move on.
- People control who can DM them, who can call them, who can see their profile, whether they appear online, and whether video exists on their account at all. Those settings are enforced by the server. Trying to get around them is a rules violation.

## 3. No scams. None.

HuSH runs automated pattern checks on messages, posts, and profiles, looking for the scammer playbook, and an admin reviews the flags. The playbook includes:

- Asking people to move the conversation off HuSH (WhatsApp, Telegram, etc.)
- Payment or crypto links, gift-card or wire requests, asking anyone for money
- Investment pitches and "guaranteed returns"
- Asking for verification codes, passwords, or harvested personal data (full name, address, occupation, income, photos)
- Romance scripts mixed with money, fake military/deployed/stranded stories
- Lottery-winner claims and giveaways of "winnings" — including pretending to be a known lottery winner
- "Your prize is ready, just pay the processing/delivery fee first" — fee-to-release is one of the heaviest signs there is
- Mass identical first-contact DMs to many people in a short window
- Names built to impersonate: rank/title claims ("General…", "Prince…"), celebrity names, known fake-profile and impersonated-winner names

The ladder is: **more than 5 signs = a formal warning** recorded on the account. **Cross the line again after a warning and the account is permanently banned** — identifiers and IP blacklisted.

One script skips the ladder entirely: if your first three questions to a brand-new contact are, in order, *how old are you*, *where are you from*, and *what are you looking for here* — that is a scammer opener, not a conversation, and the account is banned on the spot.

Names and patterns are signs, not proof — a name alone never bans anyone, and the admin reviews flagged accounts. But do not dress like the playbook and act surprised when it is treated like the playbook.

## 4. No harassment, no impersonation, no trolling

- No targeted harassment, slur spam, baiting, or flooding rooms and DMs.
- Do not impersonate real people — including public figures and lottery winners.
- Do not post other people's private information (their email, phone, address, or anything they did not make public themselves).

## 5. Rooms and cyphers

- Room chat is live and public to the room. Behave like you are in a room with people.
- Cypher rooms run on respect for the mic: get in the queue, wait your turn, pass the mic when your bars are done. The host runs their cypher; flooding or sabotaging a session gets you treated as a troll.
- Hosts can set their room's tone within these rules. The rules in this document override any room-level custom.

## 6. Your content, your responsibility

What you post, upload, and perform is yours — and your responsibility. Do not post anything illegal, anything that belongs to someone else as if it were yours, or anything designed to defraud another user.

## 7. Enforcement

- Enforcement is server-side and logged, with the evidence (including IP addresses) attached to each flag, warning, and ban for admin review.
- Bans are permanent unless the admin lifts one under the mistake/server-error exception.
- If the rules change, the version number and date at the top change, and you will be asked to accept the new version.
